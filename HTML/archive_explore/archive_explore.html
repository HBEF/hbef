Use this tool to look up barcode and bin numbers in the HBEF archives. You can filter rows using the buttons beside each column name. You can sort by clicking column names.

Not all HBEF chemistry samples are barcoded. For the full analytical dataset, see:

Hubbard Brook Watershed Ecosystem Record (HBWatER). 2024. Continuous precipitation and stream chemistry data, Hubbard Brook Ecosystem Study, 1963 – ongoing. ver 10. Environmental Data Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/d2134b69e922988cb056a3c1a837e459.
